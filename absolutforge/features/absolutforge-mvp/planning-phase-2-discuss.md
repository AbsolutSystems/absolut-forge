## Phase goal

Deliver an adaptive, code-aware discovery workflow that produces one accepted
Feature Brief with immutable intent, explicit assumptions, observable outcomes,
amendments, and links to durable ADRs.

## Scope

### In scope

- Adaptive product and architecture discussion.
- Current-state evidence gathered from code.
- Complete Feature Brief generation and one explicit acceptance gate.
- ADR creation for decisions with durable architectural consequences.
- Brief status transition from `Draft` to `Ready`.

### Out of scope

- Detailed tasks, file-by-file implementation instructions, and Execution Maps.
- QA enrichment, plan review, or section-by-section acceptance gates.
- Implementation work.

### Deliberately not doing

- A mandatory one-question-per-turn conversation protocol.
- Automatically generated acceptance-criteria taxonomies.
