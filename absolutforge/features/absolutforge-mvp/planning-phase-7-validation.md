## Phase goal

Validate manifest and skill correctness, exercise representative behavior, and
test the product thesis through a small comparative pilot against classic
AbsolutPowers.

## Scope

### In scope

- Deterministic manifest, frontmatter, path, handoff, and forbidden-ceremony
  checks.
- Behavioral scenarios for all six skills and the artifact lifecycle.
- Installation, enable/disable, and workflow documentation for Claude and Codex.
- A 3-5 change pilot measuring token use, elapsed time, handoffs, final-review
  blockers, human findings, intent fidelity, and resumability.

### Out of scope

- CI execution of budget-consuming behavioral model tests on every commit.
- Claiming universal superiority from a small pilot.
- Pi/Grok compatibility testing.

### Deliberately not doing

- Building a large evaluation platform before the workflow proves useful.
- Optimizing prompts solely for one hard-coded model.

## Assumptions and decisions

### Assumptions
